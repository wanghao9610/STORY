# Committee

**Language:** English | [简体中文](committee.zh-CN.md)

## Filling guide

- `Role`: free text using the institution's official role name. Common values are `Advisor`, `Chair`, `Co-chair`, `Member`, and `External examiner`; add repeated rows when needed and remove roles the confirmed program does not require.
- `Name`: empty until confirmed, then the person's exact required display name as free text.
- `Affiliation`: empty or the confirmed official institution/unit as free text.
- `Confirmed`: `no` or `yes` only. Keep `no` until the author has verified the row; use `yes` only when the role, name, and affiliation are all confirmed.
- `Notes`: empty or concise free text for roster facts such as a required capacity or representation. Put decisions and received feedback in the applicable milestone instead.
