# Copy-edit the dissertation

Read `docs/mds/story-workflow/writing-workflow-conventions.md` first. Resolve a chapter target or explicitly confirm a whole-dissertation pass. Load `notes/style.md`, `notes/notation.md`, `notes/story.md`, and the relevant outline briefs.

Improve sentence structure, paragraph flow, signposting, terminology, acronym introduction, and cross-chapter consistency. For a whole-dissertation pass, identify repeated paper-style introductions, duplicated related work, conflicting notation, and missing transitions.

Do not change any number, citation key, `\todo`, claim strength, contribution attribution, or technical meaning. Report those issues and route them to the owning skill. Preserve one sentence per source line.

Build after editing and write any durable follow-up as a checkbox under `tasks/`. A `style` mode may update `notes/style.md` without editing prose.
