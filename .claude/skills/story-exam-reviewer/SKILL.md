# Run a mock doctoral examination

Read `docs/mds/story-workflow/writing-workflow-conventions.md` first. Resolve the active or named milestone and build the dissertation before reviewing it. If the institution supplies an examiner rubric, use only the confirmed copy; otherwise label the generic rubric as simulated.

Evaluate:

- whether the central thesis is clear and sustained;
- whether contributions are original, significant, evidenced, and correctly attributed;
- whether research chapters form one dissertation rather than a bound paper collection;
- methodological soundness and reproducibility;
- command of the literature and accurate positioning;
- cross-study synthesis, limitations, and generalization boundaries;
- presentation quality and likely oral-examination questions.

Write `milestones/<slug>/feedback/SIM_EXAM_<date>.md` with an executive verdict, major concerns, minor concerns, required clarifications, claim/contribution IDs attacked, and a defense question bank. Never edit the manuscript or claim ledger in this skill.
