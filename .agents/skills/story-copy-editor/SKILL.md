# Copy-edit the thesis

Read `docs/mds/story-workflow/writing-workflow-conventions.md` and `docs/mds/story-workflow/human-writing-guide.md` first. Resolve a chapter target or explicitly confirm a whole-thesis pass; a whole-thesis pass includes `manus/fronts/` and `manus/backs/`, and a single front- or back-matter file is also a valid target. Load `notes/style.md` where present, plus `notes/notation.md`, `notes/story.md`, and the relevant outline briefs. Read author-confirmed style samples named by the profile. A missing style profile does not block ordinary copy-editing; missing narrative, notation, or outline metadata routes to its owning creator.

Before editing, mark protected content: facts, numbers, dates, quotations, citation keys, source comments, claim and uncertainty boundaries, technical distinctions, and attribution. Improve sentence structure, paragraph flow, signposting, terminology, acronym introduction, and cross-chapter consistency. Diagnose formulaic prose from clusters and function, not isolated words or punctuation. Rewrite around a paragraph's main proposition instead of patching individual watched phrases. For a whole-thesis pass, identify repeated paper-style introductions, duplicated related work, recurring paragraph templates, generic contribution or outlook language, conflicting notation, and missing transitions.

Do not change any number, citation key, `\todo`, `% src:` comment, claim strength, uncertainty, contribution attribution, or technical meaning. Do not add concrete detail, personal opinion, or stylistic irregularity merely to sound human. Preserve necessary hedging and deliberate rhetoric. Report issues outside prose ownership and route them to the owning skill. Preserve one sentence per source line.

After editing, compare the revision with the source and restore any protected content that moved, disappeared, or became stronger. Run `bash execs/run.sh`, then `bash execs/scpts/lint.sh --no-build`; treat prose findings as advisory and judge them against the guide's false-positive safeguards. Write any durable follow-up as a checkbox under `tasks/`.

A `style` mode may update `notes/style.md` without editing prose. In that mode, if the file is absent, create it with fields for manuscript language, authorial voice, author-confirmed sample paths, sentence and paragraph rhythm, terminology rules, acronym policy, chapter-opening pattern, transition preferences, first-person policy, qualification and claim-strength practice, punctuation practice, preserved quirks, cross-chapter signposting, citation style, and project-specific watch patterns. Manuscript language is `en | zh`; every other field is empty or author-confirmed free text, and `none` marks only a confirmed absence of a rule. Create `notes/style.zh-CN.md` in the same change and never overwrite an existing profile with the scaffold.
